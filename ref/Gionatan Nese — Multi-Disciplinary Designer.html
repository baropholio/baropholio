Gionatan Nese — Multi-Disciplinary Designer

Multi-disciplinary designer based in Italy. Gionatan Nese crafts websites, brand identities, and visual experiences that leave an impression.

Selected work

  • Tengile MalaMala Collection

    Website Design

    A new digital home for the Tengile MalaMala Collection, bringing together two distinctive names in South African safari. We created a refined, immersive website that balances MalaMala’s rich heritage with Tengile’s contemporary spirit, using considered typography, editorial structure and photography-led design to capture the character, landscape and unique sense of place of the collection.

  • Gionatan Nese, Website & Identity

    Website Design, Visual Identity

    The personal branding and portfolio website explore the relationship between identity, design, and digital craftsmanship. Through a combination of refined interactions, immersive WebGL experiences, subtle 3D elements, and carefully curated typography, the project transforms a portfolio into a memorable digital experience that reflects both personality and creative vision.

  • Anchor

    Website & Identity

    A brand identity for Anchor Apparel, from visual language to tone of voice, translated into a t-shirt line that brings the brand's personality to life.

  • Ferrari 330 P4 Concept

    Design Exploration

    An immersive digital tribute to the Ferrari 330 P4, reimagining the iconic racing car's heritage through storytelling, motion, and interaction.

  • Erica Basile

    Creative Direction

    A visual identity inspired by growth, tracing a seed into a flower across the pages to echo the book's themes of transformation.
